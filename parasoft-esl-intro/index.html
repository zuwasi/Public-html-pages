WHY IT MATTERS · 02

Regulated software must prove itself

Regulators demand evidence, and evidence needs the right tools.

Aerospace
  • DO-178C governs airborne software, with DO-330 for tool qualification
Automotive
  • ISO 26262 demands safety analyses tied to the code itself
Medical
  • IEC 62304 and FDA rules govern device software evidence
Defence
  • Programmes demand assurance at the same depth, often air-gapped
Embedded C and C++ must be verified, not just tested.
