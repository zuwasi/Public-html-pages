The asset the bank actually cares about

One cryptographic asset, walked down from the business: a retail-bank payment flow whose TLS handshake uses ECDHE P-256 key exchange, so traffic recorded today can be decrypted once a cryptographically relevant quantum computer exists (harvest-now-decrypt-later); the RSA-2048 certificate authenticates the connection and is instead a harvest-now-forge-later integrity asset. This is the kind of chain a crypto-inventory platform like q.security's discovers; the planner decides which chains get fixed in the next wave. Exposure model: Gupta & Mittal 2026 (Cybersecurity 9:147), midline GRI-2024 Q-day distribution, start 2026, 6-year migration.
