BACKGROUND

What is the Cyber Resilience Act?

EU Regulation 2024/2847 — mandatory cybersecurity requirements for all connected products on the EU market, covering the entire lifecycle.

Scope

All connected hardware and software: IoT, firmware, SaaS, libraries.

Two Key Areas

Product cybersecurity (Annex I §1) + vulnerability handling (Annex I §2).

Full Lifecycle

From design through development, delivery, and maintenance.

Enforcement

Non-compliance means market exclusion and fines up to €15M or 2.5% of revenue.

“If everything is connected, everything can be hacked.”— Ursula von der Leyen, 2021

SCOPE & TIMELINE

Who Must Comply — and When?

Who is affected?

  • Manufacturers of connected products (hardware + software)
  • Importers and distributors in the EU market
  • Open-source software stewards (light-touch regime)
  • Any company placing connected products on the EU market — globally

Key Dates

  • Sept 2026: Vulnerability & incident reporting begins
  • Dec 2027: Full application — all requirements apply
  • Products placed on market after this date must comply
