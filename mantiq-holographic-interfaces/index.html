The paper · 02

Four results that invite independent checking.

Energy scattering across a conformal interface, studied through AdSd-sliced domain walls: a short letter with dense, checkable mathematics.

1 · Closed-form transmission from the warp factor

For metrics ds² = dy² + a²(y) ds²(AdSd) asymptotic to empty AdSd+1, the transmission coefficient TL is a closed-form functional of the warp factor a(y) alone (Eq. 15), independent of the wave details.

CLOSED FORM · CHECKABLE SYMBOLICALLY

2 · Recovers d = 2, extends to general d

Reproduces the known two-dimensional holographic results (Refs. 11, 12, 16) and extends them to arbitrary dimension. The d = 2 Janus case has a fully explicit form (Eq. 154).

KNOWN RESULTS = READY-MADE REGRESSION TARGETS

3 · Universality within the class

Transmission is independent of the incidence angle, of the profile of the incident perturbation, and of the source used to create it. Examples: thin-brane models, Janus geometries (Eqs. 27 to 29).

SWEEPABLE IN A SIMULATOR

4 · Bounds from CFT-like data

Transmission is bounded by central-charge-like quantities: lower and upper bounds controlled by CTL / CTR (Eq. 23), with the bound structure proved in Appendix B. Complete reflection is attainable in multi-brane models.

INEQUALITY · A NATURAL PROOF TARGET

Key d = 2 check value (Eq. 154): TL = Ω(b) = √(b(2−b)) / (2 arctanh √(b/(2−b))), with T → 1 as b → 0 and T → 0 as b approaches the bound of the allowed range. Boundary: results are for the stated class, linearized perturbations; the Discussion itself asks how far they extend.

Fit rationale · 04

Why the front of the pipeline fits this paper.

One central object, one headline functional

The whole letter lives on the warp factor a(y) and the functional TL[a]. Closed forms, limits, and bounds around a single object are exactly what an executable oracle is for.

Known results become regression vectors

The d = 2 results (Refs. 11, 12, 16) and Eq. 154 are fixed targets. The REED pattern applies directly: freeze vectors, audit them, and never edit the oracle to fit an implementation.

The bound is a scoped proof target

Appendix B is an inequality over a stated class of warp factors, the kind of statement Lean 4 + Mathlib handles well when the scope is explicit.

Culture match

The paper already separates what is proved, what is checked, and what is expected (Appendix D is labeled a consistency check, not a fix). MantiQ formalizes exactly that separation.

Boundary: this is the assessment for the workflow's first four stages. Stage-fit for the endpoint is addressed separately (slides 07 and 09).

Stage 03 · Wolfram · 05

The notebook: an independent oracle for TL.

Symbolic re-derivations

  • Evaluate the warp-factor integral (Eq. 15) for the thin-brane family and the explicit per-dimension forms of Appendix F.
  • Reproduce the d = 2 Janus closed form (Eq. 154) from the warp-factor solution (Eq. 29).

Limit and edge behavior

  • b → 0 gives TL = 1 (no defect); b approaches its maximum gives TL → 0 (complete reflection, logarithmic divergence).
  • Pure-AdS reference: TL = 1 by construction.

Dimension scans

  • Sweep even and odd d, testing the paper's observation that transmission increases slightly with d under the b / bmax parameterization.
  • Check the tension bounds of Appendix C against the admissible-parameter region.

Frozen vectors and audit

  • Deterministic regression vectors (d, family, parameter, TL) with SHA-256 manifest, the REED vector-schema pattern.
  • A named audit of checks, run headless via wolframscript, with exact counts reported.

Status language: each item ends as SYMBOLICALLY VALIDATED for the named families and cases only. A notebook checks named cases; it is not a universal theorem.

Stage 04 · Lean 4 · 06

Scoped theorems, published boundary.

Candidate headline theorems

  • Bounds: for the stated warp-factor class, TL lies between the CTL / CTR-controlled lower and upper bounds (the Appendix B inequality structure).
  • d = 2 certificate: the Janus integral evaluates to Ω(b) = √(b(2−b)) / (2 arctanh √(b/(2−b))), kernel-checked like the REED BASE-1024 certificate.
  • Complete reflection: TL → 0 as b approaches the allowed maximum, under stated hypotheses.

What Lean would not prove

  • The linearized Einstein-scalar derivation (Appendix A) and holographic renormalization stay outside.
  • Israel-condition matching (Appendix D) and the angular-coordinate computations stay outside.
  • These remain PAPER / DERIVED, with the derivation chain cited. Declared non-goals, not omissions.

Trust discipline

No sorry, no admit, no project axioms. Only Mathlib's logical foundation (propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound), with an axiom audit published alongside the theorems, as in the REED and QPE runs.

Precedent scope

The QPE case study proved exactly one resource identity and said so. The same honesty applies here: a bounded, correct proof set beats an implied but unreachable whole-paper formalization.

Boundary: Lean proves mathematics under definitions. It asserts nothing about any numerical solver, and the solver asserts nothing about Lean.

Stage 05 · Simulator · 07

The endpoint for a theory paper: a scattering lab.

What it computes

Numerically solve the linearized perturbation equation (the Eq. 8 wave equation) on domain-wall backgrounds, extract reflected and transmitted energy flux from the asymptotics, and compare against the closed-form TL.

Cross-model evidence

Agreement between two independent models (numerical evolution vs the Wolfram oracle) is the evidence class, mirroring Wolfram-vs-Python in REED and browser-vs-Wolfram in QPE. Disagreement is preserved and diagnosed, never repaired away.

What it makes tangible

Choose d, family (thin brane, Janus, multi-brane), and parameters; watch the transmission curve move, watch the bounds tighten as CTL / CTR changes, and see the slight d-dependence directly.

The honest endpoint

This paper defines no deployable artifact, so there is no FPGA or contract stage. The pipeline ends where the QPE case study ends: a runnable public lab plus an evidence package. That is the correct shape here, not a limitation.

Status language: simulator agreement is NUMERICALLY TESTED for the swept grid. It is not exhaustive and it does not upgrade any symbolic or formal claim.

Verdict · 09

Is this paper a good fit? Yes, with one scope adjustment.

● Strong fit

Wolfram oracle for the closed forms, limits, and dimension scans. Claim and ambiguity ledgers for a conventions-heavy holography derivation. Independent numerical cross-check through the simulator.

● Adapted fit

Lean 4 verification must be scoped: bounds, certificates, and limits are realistic; formalizing the full linearized-gravity derivation is research-level work and stays a declared non-goal.

● Not applicable

The RTL, FPGA, and Solidity gates do not apply: the letter defines no deployable artifact. The endpoint is a public evidence package, QPE-shaped rather than REED-shaped.

Pipeline coverage: stages 01 to 06 fully applicable Hardware gate: absent by nature of the paper, not skipped Effort shape: comparable to the QPE case study, plus a deeper Lean stage

Summary: roughly the first two thirds of the MantiQ pipeline (normalize, compute, verify, simulate) apply directly and strongly; the last third (implementation hardware) is replaced by the simulator endpoint and the evidence package.

Value · 10

What the authors gain from a run.

Independent confirmation of the core formula

TL[a], the bounds, and the limit behavior re-derived by a separate symbolic engine, formalized where feasible, and tested numerically. Useful before follow-up work builds on the letter.

A public, extensible artifact

Notebook, Lean files, vectors, and simulator travel together with reproduction instructions, like the QPE evidence folder. Readers and referees can re-run rather than re-derive.

A lab for the open questions

The Discussion asks how far universality extends: more general matter couplings, top-down solutions, beyond linearized order. The simulator is the instrument for those probes.

A precise claimed-versus-proved map

The claim-status table gives referees and readers an exact picture of what is proved, what is computed, and what is assumed, strengthening rather than inflating the paper's claims.

Note: nothing about the paper's results changes. What changes is how independently a reader can verify them, and how easily follow-up work can start from a checked baseline.
