01 / The buying decision

Three security questions.
Not one interchangeable market.

CSPM + CNAPP

Is our cloud exposed?

Discover misconfigurations, risky access, attack paths and threats to running workloads.

Primary buyer: cloud security, platform engineering and the SOC.

BLACK DUCK SCA / POLARIS

Can we govern our code?

Find open-source components, licensing obligations and vulnerabilities. Polaris adds broader application security testing.

Primary buyer: AppSec, development and open-source program teams.

ESL SBOMATOR

Can we explain our product?

Connect software, firmware, hardware, AI and dataset evidence to remediation and regulated-product review.

Primary buyer: product security, embedded engineering and quality/regulatory teams.

Positioning: SBOMator is a product supply-chain evidence platform and a candidate SCA alternative, not a blanket replacement for CNAPP.

Category-level synthesis, not an exhaustive ranking. CNAPP and AppSec products increasingly overlap in code, AI and supply-chain security. [S1–S5]

04 / Master plan treated as complete

Scenario assumption, not a release audit

From finding more risk
to making remediation reviewable.

F1–F4 / REMEDIATION CONTEXT

What can be fixed?

Fixed versions, fix age, CVE vintage, earliest-disclosure provenance and separate, low-confidence signals for security updates without CVEs.

F5–F7 / PREVENTION

What should we admit?

Container/Yocto/firmware removal candidates, package-version cooling periods, malicious-package signals and verified CI/CD references.

F8–F10 / ACCOUNTABILITY

What supports the decision?

Recomputed severity, source disagreements, human-reviewed AI/SARIF intake and grouped remediation plans with ownership and due dates.

F11–F14 / OPERATIONS

What changed?

Readiness indicators, board metrics, memory-safety/crypto exposure and deterministic policy gates across GUI and CLI.

STEPS 21–24 / EVIDENCE

What must be retained?

CRA reporting preparation, EUVD/EOL/retention evidence, FDA eSTAR artifacts, risk-review context and consistent presentation.

STEPS 25–26 / COVERAGE

Was the scan complete?

Customer accuracy fixes, resolved dependency graphs, per-source scan ledgers, fallback analysis and explicit incomplete-coverage states.

Scope: F1–F14 plus the master plan’s step index through Step 26. Removal recommendations require rebuild/re-scan; AI suggestions require approval; offline unknowns never mean clean. [S1]

06 / Black Duck, specifically

A credible SCA competitor.
Not a cloud-only straw man.

BLACK DUCK’S DOCUMENTED STRENGTHS

Deep open-source governance

  • Multiple discovery techniques: dependencies, source, snippets and binaries.
  • Curated KnowledgeBase, Black Duck Security Advisories and license intelligence.
  • SBOM exchange, policy enforcement, SDLC integrations and continuous monitoring.
  • On-premises, hosted and air-gapped choices; Polaris for SaaS AppSec.
SBOMATOR’S EVALUATION CASE

Connected product evidence

  • Evaluate embedded build context, firmware and hardware/software relationships.
  • Combine AI/agent inventory and local DataBOM with product evidence.
  • Carry fix age, disclosure provenance and human decisions into CRA/FDA packages.
  • Offer transparent site/user pricing as the commercial starting point.
The honest challenge: use the same product build, known component list and required evidence pack. Measure results and analyst effort before claiming superiority.

Black Duck also markets AI model scanning and software supply-chain security. SBOMator’s AI story is not an exclusivity claim. Curated-data depth and enterprise operating fit must be evaluated, not presumed. [S1, S3–S5]

08 / Data sovereignty and air-gap

Local control is valuable.
Fresh intelligence still has a cost.

SBOMATOR

Keep evidence close

Local scans, DataBOM and fingerprint-based provenance can avoid content upload. External AI, registry lookups and public provenance search are separate connected choices.

Verify: disabled network paths, feed transfer, freshness, redaction and behavior when metadata is missing.

BLACK DUCK

Air-gap is an option

Official documentation includes Detect air-gap archives and an Air-gapped KnowledgeBase.

Documented limit: its air-gapped KB lacks the big-data infrastructure behind file-level license data, copyright data and actual license text.

CSPM / CNAPP

Live context needs access

Cloud posture depends on cloud APIs and permissions; runtime modules may require sensors, telemetry or logs.

Verify: read permissions, snapshot handling, data residency, retention and incident-response access.

“Offline” is a deployment property, not a promise that every online enrichment remains available.

SBOMator marks unavailable checks unknown/not evaluated. AI-assisted triage is not assumed offline or included in the base price. Black Duck limitation is specific to its documented air-gapped KB, not all deployments. [S1, S2, S8, S9]

09 / The regulated-product opportunity

Cloud compliance checks are not
a product technical file.

EU CRA / PRODUCT LIFECYCLE

Prepare and retain

SBOM/VEX, support-period and end-of-life context, evidence index, retention manifest and vulnerability decisions.

Prepare SRP fields and paste sheets, track reporting deadlines, and record submission history. No automatic ENISA API submission claim.

FDA / MEDICAL DEVICES

Organize reviewable artifacts

SBOM Support Report, Unresolved Anomalies, Measures and Metrics, asset/interface inventory and Section 524B evidence.

Keep CVSS alongside configurable, manufacturer-reviewed TIR57 risk context. Hardware and dataset evidence complement the software record.

Why it matters commercially: evaluate how much manual evidence assembly remains after scanning, not only how many findings appear on a dashboard.

Completed-plan scenario. Manufacturer validation, clinical context, risk acceptance, patch delivery, formal reporting and regulatory approval remain human responsibilities. Evidence preparation is not certification or legal advice. The later master-plan addendum supersedes older brochure automation wording. [S1]

14 / Proof-of-value

Make the comparison reproducible.

ONE CONTROLLED INPUT SET

Use the actual product

One source commit, lock/resolved graphs, the shipped binary or firmware, a container and required hardware/dataset artifacts.

Freeze tool versions and intelligence timestamps. Include known-present, absent, backported and deliberately unresolved cases.

FOUR MEASURABLE OUTPUTS

Score results, not slide counts

  • Component and license correctness against reviewed ground truth.
  • False positives, missed findings and explicit scan-coverage gaps.
  • Time to an approved remediation/VEX decision.
  • Completeness of the required evidence package and all-in quote.
For CNAPP, add a separate cloud test: seeded misconfiguration, excess privilege and supported runtime-threat scenarios in an authorized test account. An SBOM test cannot establish cloud-protection parity.

Suggested evaluation protocol, not a completed benchmark. No live customer data or proprietary competitor tests are embedded in this presentation.
