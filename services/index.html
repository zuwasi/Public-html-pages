The MantiQ Factor

MantiQ Factor is my product vision and engineering workflow for converting research and technical documents into traceable, formally verified executable systems. It combines structured document conversion, AI orchestration, Wolfram computation, Lean 4 proof, domain skills, curated knowledge, and code or hardware synthesis.

The goal is not automatic code generation without controls. Every stage is designed to remain inspectable, reproducible, and connected to its source evidence.
